What Are Industrial Distribution Centers?

Distribution centers store goods from manufacturers and wholesalers before sending them to clients or customers. Most distribution centers are built strategically to fulfill a customer’s order quickly.

What Are the Types of Industrial Facilities?

You can find several types of industrial buildings that are considered distribution centers. Let’s look at some of them.

Distribution Centers

These centers store goods ready for use, aiming to pack and ship them to their final destination as quickly as possible. Distribution centers are also referred to as fulfillment centers, as they tend to add order fulfillment.

Distribution centers are commonly confused with warehouses and warehouse construction, and it’s easy to see why. Both are used to store goods. However, a warehouse stores goods indefinitely, while distribution centers hold goods briefly to reach their destination.

Distribution centers store many types of goods. You may find cold storage distribution centers, similar to refrigerated warehouses, that keep the goods cold before delivery.

Manufacturing Plants

A manufacturing plant creates products that businesses use. Many manufacturing plants will work for a specific industry, such as creating electronics. They can be ideal if you need goods and parts for less, as they tend to have a high output for little cost.
