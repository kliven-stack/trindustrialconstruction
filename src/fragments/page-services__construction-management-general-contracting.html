THE DIFFERNCE BETWEEN CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT AND GENERAL CONTRACTING

CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT:

The management of commercial and industrial construction is a professional service that involves overseeing, coordinating, and managing construction projects. This service is often utilized for complex large-scale projects.

GENERAL CONTRACTING:

Unlike Commercial and Industrial Construction Management, Commercial and Industrial General Contracting is a type of contracting that concentrates on the physical execution of construction. General contractors are accountable for managing construction projects daily, including organizing workers, materials, and equipment. Despite their critical roles in the achievement of construction projects, commercial and industrial construction management and general contracting are unique services with different objectives.
