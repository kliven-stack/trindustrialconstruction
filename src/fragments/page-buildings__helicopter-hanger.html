Helicopter Hangar Construction: Essential Design and Building Tips for Success

Key Factors to Consider in Helicopter Hangar Design

When constructing helicopter or aircraft hangars, you must consider five key factors to ensure the safest experience. Everything, from the overhead cranes to the foundation, needs consideration. Let’s look at five of them.

Size and Layout Specifications

When it comes to aircraft storage, the number one consideration is fitting the aircraft inside of it. Airplane hangars are constructed based on several factors. Wingspan and how many aircraft you’ll store are among the most crucial concerns. Usually, hangar doors will be as long as the wingspan, plus five feet. You do not want to have a tight squeeze, and having additional space can be helpful for your staff. Knowing your ideal layout for an aircraft hangar helps make construction smoother.

Door System Selection

Having the ideal large doors for your aircraft to enter is also vital. Some hangars choose a single door, while others want multiple doors for multiple helicopters. The design of the door is also a question you may want to ask, as you want something that will fit your building’s aesthetic. Durable, flexible doors that open and close fast are what you need, and a professional can offer the best ones for your money.

Climate Control and Ventilation

Heat is a challenge for many metal buildings, including aircraft hangars. Having proper cooling units available can prevent your aircraft from overheating. On the other hand, during freezing weather conditions, you want a hangar that will keep your aircraft warm and ready to depart.

In addition, you need to have proper ventilation that meets the legal requirements and keeps your workers safe. A professional can give you the best cost-effective climate control and ventilation solution.

Lighting and Electrical Needs

For your pilots’ and workers’ sake, having a well-lit aircraft hangar can benefit everyone. Another consideration is the location of your outlets. Ideally, you do not want outlets that require extension cords too far, as this can be a hassle and a possible danger. Electrical outlets near rafters and other convenient areas can be a valuable solution. A close location that does not get in the way of the aircraft’s departure is ideal.

Should You Use Pre-Engineered Metal for Helicopter Hangars?

When choosing building materials for your hangar, many look at pre-engineered metal. While pre-engineered buildings have some constraints regarding expansion, the pros far outweigh the cons. Let’s look at why more people choose pre-engineered metal building options and what the leading manufacturer can bring you.


Customizable Design Options

You can choose from several options when constructing your pre-engineered metal building, such as rooftop design, door appearance, layout, and more. With these buildings, making one that is more customizable to you for less is much easier.

Cost and Time Savings

Constructing a helicopter hangar can be expensive and take many hours. However, a pre-engineered metal building takes less time to construct, and the cost is a fraction of what ordinary metal buildings cost. Despite this, pre-engineered metal buildings do not sacrifice quality, keeping your aircraft safe.

Longevity and Low Maintenance

Pre-engineered metal aircraft hangars may last much longer than regular metal hangars. One reason is that pre-engineered metal buildings are much more resistant to rust, rotting, mold, mildew, and pests. You’ll spend less time maintaining the building and more improving it.

Navigating Helicopter Hangar Construction Regulations

When searching for the ideal helicopter hangar, you must consider regulations, which can depend on local, state, and federal laws.

Local Zoning and Building Codes

Everything from the hangar’s size to the ventilation placement has codes, along with its location. These codes are here for your safety and the safety of others around you. An ideal construction company will ensure that your hangar meets these requirements.

FAA Requirements

The FAA also has many requirements on how you use your hangar. An ideal construction company will explain these to you and ensure you are legally clear before construction.

Environmental and Noise Considerations

Your hangar also needs to meet environmental and noise laws. These laws are here to preserve the ecosystem and to ensure that the locals are not disturbed by the sound of your aircraft.
