How Do You Select the Appropriate Type of Car Wash Facility?

While it may seem simple, selecting the right location for your car wash requires thorough planning. But the location will choose exactly what type of facility you should erect for your car wash design.

Here is how to find the best areas for your car wash construction:

Near Your Customers

The best place to set up a car wash business is customers will pass on a daily basis. Offices complexes, construction sites, and laundromats are great suggestions because they allow the customer multitask their car’s wash with another task.

Malls and Offices

Placing a self serve car wash by a mall is highly profitable because of the sheer traffic. Tunnel car washes are also always ideal for a bay close to your users. Consider offering discounted costs to employees of nearby businesses.

Main Roads

Car washes situated around main roads generate high revenue because of the added convenience. So, not only can you expect customer loyalty, your location also brings more customers if your tunnel car wash is cost-effective and around their daily commute.

Partnership in High-traffic Areas

It isn’t uncommon to see tunnel car washes in gas stations and huge mall complexes. This is because it is an opportunity to plug a successful car wash in an area that receives hundreds of drivers every daily.

What Permits and Licenses are Necessary for a Car Wash Business?

In today’s age, a business without the necessary certifications, permits and licenses hardly receives as much patronage. This is because clients seek to trust a business and are only able to do so when there are licenses to back the claims up.

You’ll need:

  • Environmental regulations
  • Business name registration
  • General business permit or license
  • Certificate of occupancy
  • Federal employer identification number
  • Entity formation
  • Business number or sales tax number

What Are Environmental Regulations?

Environmental regulations are a set of rules that control the activities of businesses as it relates to the environment.

They are particularly essential for car wash businesses because car washes discharge wastewater which can potentially contaminate the air, land, and ground water. So, car wash businesses that don’t follow regulations are waiting to be penalized or even shut down.
